A quick heads-up for those of you that find yourself using whiteboards a lot. [Whiteboard Capture](http://www.beetlebugsoftware.com/index.html) is a $1.99 iPhone app designed to help you make those whiteboard photos you take significantly more readable (and, therefore, more useful as reference).
