Arguably the best linguistic "style guide" ever written for contemporary English. Doubly-relevant to _Uncarved_, since it both informs the way I aim to write, and serves as a canon for how sentences and paragraphs can most optimally be constructed.

> Vigorous writing is concise. A sentence should contain no unnecessary words, a paragraph no unnecessary sentences, for the same reason that a drawing should have no unnecessary lines and a machine no unnecessary parts.
<span class="attribution">William Strunk Jr., _The Elements of Style, 1918_</span>

There is a [new hardcover edition](http://www.amazon.com/Elements-Style-50th-Anniversary/dp/0205632645/ref=pd_bbs_3?ie=UTF8&s=books&qid=1237906050&sr=8-3) to commemorate the anniversary. [via [Daring Fireball](http://daringfireball.net)]
