In contrast with the spirit of yesterday's link to Designing Convertbots application comes news of the [confirmed](http://www.techcrunch.com/2009/04/01/is-office-finally-coming-to-the-iphone/) continuation of effort to bring Microsoft Office to the iPhone.

Absent an iPhone OS device with a significantly larger screen, I can't imagine any purpose to cramming the UI of Word (or Excel!) into as space with the iPhone's screen real estate. If Microsoft had _any_ track record for pulling off surprisingly excellent (or even simple and focused) UI solutions, I'd leave room for a verdict.

Who knows? Maybe they'll actually impress this time.
