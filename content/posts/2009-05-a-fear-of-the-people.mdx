The Iranian government has [blocked access to Facebook](http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/middle_east/8065578.stm). This in the run up to its June 12 presidential elections.

The blessing hiding behind the headline to this story is that this action boldly demonstrates just how much Ahmadinejad's administration _fears_ the thought of what may be possible if its governed citizens have the opportunity to communicate with each other in such a public and open forum.

Good luck ousting the douche bag, Iran.
