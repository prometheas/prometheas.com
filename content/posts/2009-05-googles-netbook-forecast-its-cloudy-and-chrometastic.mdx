Jason Kincaid [writes](http://www.techcrunch.com/2009/05/07/eric-schmidt-on-netbooks-forget-android-its-all-about-cloud-services/) about the insights of Google CEO Eric Schmidt:

> [Schmidt] talked about Netbooks... saying the the unit numbers were becoming material and that use cases were “consistent with the cloud computing model” that Google is focusing on with Google Docs, Gmail and other services.

And this isn't some smokescreen or distractionary tactic; Schmidt's words are true to Google's actions. Why do you suppose [Chrome](http://www.google.com/chrome) is designed to [spawn each of its tabs as a separate process on its host OS](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JGmO7Oximw8)? [jump to 1:40]

Google sees "cloud computing" (read: _Chrome_) as the platform for the netbook, not Android.

With the offline storage capabilities coming in HTML5, it seems that[web browser operating systems](http://eyeos.org/) are less laughable by the day.
