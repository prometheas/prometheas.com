In January of 1998, Netscape — in a last-ditch effort to retaliate against Microsoft's domination of the browser market with its Internet Explorer browser — took to the strategy of [open sourcing the source code](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netscape#Open_sourcing) for their flagship product, Netscape Navigator. And so the [Mozilla Project](http://www.mozilla.com/en-US/about/) was born, which has since brought the world the [Firefox](http://getfirefox.com/) web browser, and the [Thunderbird](http://getthunderbird.com/) email client (as well a handful of [other](http://getsongbird.com/) [things](http://celtx.com/)).

And only now, at the end of December 2009, Firefox 3.5 — the latest release of the software open sourced twelve years ago — has at long last eked out ahead of any single version of rival Internet Explorer.

Source: [StatCounter Global Stats - Browser Version Market Share](http://gs.statcounter.com/#browser_version-ww-weekly-200827-200951)

It's been a long road, Mozilla; congratulations on this hard-earned milestone.
