There's something intensely satisfying about the fact that an ancient design principle can be so relevant to a modern medium.

> Since the Renaissance, many artists and architects have proportioned their works to approximate the golden ratio — especially in the form of the golden rectangle, in which the ratio of the longer side to the shorter is the golden ratio.

— [Applying Divine Proportion To Your Web Designs | How-To | Smashing Magazine](http://www.smashingmagazine.com/2008/05/29/applying-divine-proportion-to-web-design/)
